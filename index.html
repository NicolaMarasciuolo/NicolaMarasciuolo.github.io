About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at University of Trento. I work on lattice metamaterials, homogenization and continuum modeling.

My research connects discrete Bloch–Floquet analysis with enriched continuum models, micropolar and strain gradient elasticity, to capture both macro-scale and micro-scale critical modes in periodic lattice structures.

Research

Discrete Bloch–Floquet analysis
A periodic lattice can be studied through a single unit cell. Bloch–Floquet analysis gives the critical loads and the buckling modes of the whole periodic structure from that one cell.
Enriched continuum models
Micropolar and strain gradient elasticity add rotations and gradients to classical elasticity, so that the continuum still carries information about what happens inside the cell.
Macro and micro critical modes
Comparing the two descriptions shows when an instability involves the whole structure and when it stays at the scale of the cell, and whether the continuum model can predict each case.
